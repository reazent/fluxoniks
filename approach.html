How we work

Methodology + Standard + Deliverable

Every engagement leads with the standard that governs it, follows a documented methodology, and produces a specific, named deliverable. That is what makes our work auditable, repeatable and defensible — not vague advisory.

Three principles

Built for credibility, engineered for repeat delivery

Our approach rests on three commitments that make deliverables defensible — and make engagements repeatable across teams and reporting years.

Standards-first

We lead with the standard that governs the work — GHG Protocol, ISO 14064, SBTi, ISSB, CSRD, EU Taxonomy, PCAF. Methodology follows the standard; deliverables evidence it.

Assurance-ready

Deliverables are structured for third-party verification from day one — full audit trails, documented boundaries, data-quality trails and a recalculation policy. Verification should be a pass-through, not a rework cycle.

Repeatable

Same methodology, same data architecture, same deliverable structure across reporting years — so the second inventory, the third disclosure, the fourth EPD all build on the first.

Engagement

Standards we work to

The named standards behind every deliverable

We do not invent methodology. We apply the international standards that regulators, investors and procurement teams recognise — and we name them on every deliverable.

GHG Protocol

Corporate, Scope 2 & Scope 3 Standards — the global methodology for measuring and reporting corporate emissions.

ISO 14064-1 / -3

Quantification & verification of organisational GHG emissions — the verifiable backbone of a credible inventory.

SBTi

Science Based Targets initiative — corporate near-term and net-zero target setting, including FLAG for land-use sectors.

IFRS S2 / ISSB

Climate-related financial disclosures — the converging global baseline, adopted in Canada by the CSSB.

CSRD ESRS E1

EU climate reporting standard — double-materiality and the E1 climate disclosure requirements.

ISO 14067

Product carbon footprint — the bridge between full LCA and a single, defensible kg CO₂e per unit.

ISO 14040 / 14044

Life cycle assessment — the methodological backbone for product-level environmental impact.

EN 15804

Construction product EPDs — the PCR-driven framework behind procurement-ready declarations.

EU Taxonomy

Sustainable activities classification — TSC, DNSH and Minimum Safeguards for green finance eligibility.

PCAF

Financed emissions — the global standard for Scope 3 Category 15 across asset classes.

ICMA GBP / GLP

Green bond & loan principles — the four-component framework behind credible green debt instruments.

IRA / CSA G&T Bonds

Clean energy tax credits (US) and green/transition bond framework (Canada) for project eligibility.
