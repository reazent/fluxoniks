GHG Accounting & Verification

SVC-01

A complete corporate greenhouse gas inventory — Scope 1, Scope 2 (location- and market-based) and Scope 3 (all 15 value-chain categories screened, priority categories quantified). Structured to be directly verifiable under ISO 14064-3 and ready for CDP, TCFD, CSRD or SBTi submissions.
