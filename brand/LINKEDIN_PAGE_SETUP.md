# Fluxoniks — LinkedIn Company Page setup

This file has **everything** to create the LinkedIn Company Page and connect it to your
personal profile. LinkedIn pages must be created on linkedin.com (no API), so you'll do
the final steps in your browser — the assets and copy below are ready to paste.

Assets (generated in this folder):
- `linkedin-logo.png` — 400×400 square logo (upload as company logo)
- `linkedin-logo@2x.png` — 800×800 retina (use if you want extra crispness)
- `linkedin-cover.png` — 1128×376 cover banner
- `linkedin-cover@2x.png` — 2256×752 retina cover

> Pricing is intentionally omitted from all copy — it's provided on request after a
> scoping call, consistent with the website.

## 1. Create the Company Page (linkedin.com)

1. Sign in at https://www.linkedin.com with your personal profile (the one you want
   as the Page super admin).
2. Go to **https://www.linkedin.com/company/setup/new/** (or: **For Business →
   Create a Company Page → "Create"**).
3. Choose **"Company"** (not "School"/"Showcase"/"Government").
4. Fill in:

**Page identity**
- **Company name:** `Fluxoniks`
- **Language:** English
- **Public URL:** `linkedin.com/company/fluxoniks` (set "Edit URL" → `fluxoniks`)
- **Website:** `https://fluxoniks.com`
- **Industry:** `Management Consulting`
   - (Alternative that also fits: `Environmental Services`)
- **Company size:** `1-10 employees` (adjust to reality)
- **Company type:** `Privately Held`
- **Year founded:** `2024` (or the year you started)

**Logo & cover**
- Upload `linkedin-logo.png` as the **Logo**.
- Upload `linkedin-cover.png` as the **Cover image**.

5. Check the **verification** box (you're authorised to act on behalf of Fluxoniks) and
   click **Create page**.
## 2. Complete the Page → "About" tab

After the page is created, go to the **About** tab and **Edit** with the copy below.

**Tagline / Slogan** (max ~120 chars)
```
Standards-based GHG accounting, LCA, climate disclosure & sustainable finance — US & Canada.
```

**Specialties / Keywords** (LinkedIn "Specialties" field — paste as-is)

**Location / HQ**
- **Country:** United States (or Canada — your primary market)
- **Postal code:** your ZIP/postal code

**Page URL:** `https://fluxoniks.com`

Save. The Page is now live and public.
## 3. Connect the Page to YOUR personal profile

1. Open your personal LinkedIn profile → click **Edit intro** (the pencil on your
   profile header).
2. In the **Current experience** section, click **+ Add position**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Title:** `Founder` (or `Principal Consultant` / `Managing Director`)
   - **Company:** start typing `Fluxoniks` and **select the Company Page you just
     created** (it must appear as a linked Page, not free text).
   - **Employment type:** `Full-time` (or `Self-employed`)
   - **Industry:** `Management Consulting`
   - **Start date:** your start month/year
   - **Headline** (in your profile intro, not the position): consider
     `Founder at Fluxoniks — Standards-based GHG & Sustainability Consulting | US & Canada`
4. **Save**. Your profile now shows "Founder at Fluxoniks" with a clickable link to the
   Company Page.

## 4. Add a custom button on the Page (optional, ~1 min)

On the Page header, set the **Action button** to **"Visit website"** →
`https://fluxoniks.com` (or **"Request a quote"** → `https://fluxoniks.com/contact.html`).
